CarbonFactorResolver system architecture

Structured factor resolution, evidence qualification, human review and immutable locking.

  1. 1 · UpstreamDocument Intelligence / carbon-report creates a structured FactorQuery or ResolutionRequest.
  2. 2 · CFR runtimeEntity and retrieval intent resolve against hash-pinned local catalogues and validated structured sources.
  3. 3 · Safety gatesBoundary, subject, evidence, process and source-quality qualification run before ranking and explanation.
  4. 4 · Human governanceA reviewer approves an eligible recommendation or records a reasoned reference override, then locks the factor.
  5. 5 · Downstreamcarbon-report consumes the reviewed, locked factor for calculation and report generation.
  6. Offline onlyThe developer QA harness builds isolated catalogues, benchmarks and holdouts; it is not a production parser or runtime API.
Product boundary: CFR accepts structured JSON records; document parsing and formal footprint calculation remain outside the runtime.
